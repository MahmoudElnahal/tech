Architecture

How I build AI systems

Most of my systems follow the same five layers.

Channels

WhatsApp, Telegram, web forms, email and voice.

Orchestration

n8n, UiPath and FastAPI handle routing, batching, retries and webhooks.

Intelligence

LLM agents with tools and memory, tuned for Arabic.

Knowledge

Hybrid retrieval and structured data, with cited sources.

Action

Results written to the CRM, email, reports and bookings.

Deterministic where it matters

Scores and verdicts are computed by code. The LLM explains results but does not decide them.

Every answer is cited

RAG systems return the article, page or file behind each answer, and say "not found" instead of guessing.

Human review

Uncertain cases go to a person, and priority cases alert the responsible team.
