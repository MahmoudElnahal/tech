principle_01Facts come from code, not the model

Where correctness matters (hadith grading, legal articles, lead scores), the verdict is computed by deterministic rules. The LLM explains; it doesn't decide.

principle_02Every answer cites its source

My RAG systems return the article, page or file behind each answer, and they say "not found" instead of guessing.

principle_03Humans stay in the loop

Uncertain or incomplete cases are routed to a person, and hot leads trigger instant alerts, so automation supports the team rather than replacing its judgment.
